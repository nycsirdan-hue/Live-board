POST-EVENT REVIEW — USE AFTER ANY EVENT WITH A DISPLAY CHANGE

1. Did every entry remain fully visible throughout the event?
2. Was the smallest text still comfortably readable from across the room?
3. Did the board choose an effective number of columns as entries were added and removed?
4. Did the layout behave correctly for the screen and event orientation?
5. Were any names, handles, photos, or long descriptions clipped or awkwardly wrapped?
6. What worked especially well and should become the standard everywhere?
7. What needs to be changed before the next event?
8. Is the lesson universal, or genuinely specific to this event or orientation?
